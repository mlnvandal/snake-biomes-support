Snake Biomes Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Snake Biomes is developed by Vladislav Brodskii. The game does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device. They may be included in a device backup according to your Apple settings. The developer cannot access this local data.

Services and purchases

The game does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or in-app purchases. App Store downloads and device services are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

If the game's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated. For privacy questions, use the support page.
